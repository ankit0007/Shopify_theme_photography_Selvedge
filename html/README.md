# Selvedge static preview

Open `index.html` in a browser. Pages are rendered from the real Liquid theme files.

- The bar at the bottom switches between the five presets and jumps between the 19 pages.
- Try: clicking the runway photo, hovering a product card and picking a size, hovering colour dots, the heart,
  the Women menu, and "Size guide" on the product page (type your chest and waist).
- Cart, checkout, forms and filters are simulated here; on Shopify they work for real.
- Photos are CC0 images from StockSnap and rawpixel, used for the demo only.
